A Collection of Vintage Greenlee Woodworking Machinery

A working museum of cast iron and craft, housed in two purpose-built timber frame workshops.

For over a century, Greenlee Bros. & Co. of Rockford, Illinois built woodworking machinery of remarkable engineering and lasting beauty. This is a personal collection, restored and preserved — each machine returned to working order in a shop built to honor it.
